Ayoub's Ultimate Gaming PC Guide 🎮

Comprehensive knowledge for building the perfect high-end machine.

1. CPU - Central Processing Unit

The brain of the system that handles logic and game physics.

  • Best Performance: Intel Core i9-14900K.
  • Gaming King: AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D.
  • Advice: High core counts matter for streaming and productivity.

2. GPU - Graphics Processing Unit

The heart of gaming. It determines your FPS and visual quality.

  • High-End: NVIDIA RTX 4090 (24GB VRAM).
  • Value Choice: NVIDIA RTX 4070 Super or AMD RX 7800 XT.
  • Tip: Always check if your case has enough space for the GPU length.

3. RAM - System Memory

Provides fast access for active programs and games.

  • Ideal Amount: 32GB (2x16GB) is perfect for 2026.
  • Speed: Aim for DDR5-6000 CL30 for maximum stability.

4. Storage - NVMe SSD

Fast storage for lightning-quick loading times.

  • Primary Drive: 2TB M.2 NVMe SSD Gen 4/5.
  • Reliability: Samsung 990 Pro or Crucial T705.

5. Motherboard - The Backbone

Connects all your components and manages power distribution.

  • Intel Socket: LGA1700 (Z790 Chipset).
  • AMD Socket: AM5 (X670E or B650 Chipset).

6. PSU - Power Supply Unit

The "Engine" that provides stable power to your expensive parts.

  • Wattage: 850W to 1000W for high-end builds.
  • Efficiency: Look for 80 Plus Gold or Platinum rating.
  • Safety: Never go cheap on the PSU; it protects your entire PC.

7. Cooling - Air or Liquid (AIO)

Keeps your CPU from overheating under heavy load.

  • Liquid (AIO): 360mm or 420mm radiators for high-end i9/Ryzen 9.
  • Air Cooling: Noctua NH-D15 for silence and reliability.

8. PC Case - The Chassis

The shell that holds everything. Airflow is the most important feature.

  • Style: Mid-Tower or Full-Tower.
  • Airflow: Mesh front panels are best for cooling.
  • Popular: Lian Li O11 Dynamic or Corsair 4000D/5000D.

9. Monitor - The Window

Where the magic happens. High refresh rates are key for gamers.

  • Resolution: 1440p (2K) or 2160p (4K).
  • Refresh Rate: 144Hz, 240Hz, or even 360Hz for esports.
  • Panel Type: OLED is the best for color and response time.
