Ayoub's Gaming PC Guide 🎮

Your ultimate guide to understanding high-end gaming components.

1. CPU - Central Processing Unit

The "Brain" of the computer that processes game logic and physics.

Top Recommendations for 2026:

  • Intel Core i9-14900K: Best for high-end multitasking and gaming.
  • AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D: Currently the king of pure gaming performance.
  • Look for: At least 8 Cores and high clock speeds (GHz) for smooth performance.

2. GPU - Graphics Processing Unit

The most important part for gaming. It renders every frame you see on screen.

Top Recommendations for 2026:

  • NVIDIA RTX 4090: Unmatched power for 4K ultra gaming.
  • AMD Radeon RX 7900 XTX: Great performance with a large VRAM.
  • VRAM Tip: Aim for at least 12GB or 16GB of VRAM for modern AAA titles.

3. RAM - Random Access Memory

Stores data that your PC needs to access quickly while gaming or streaming.

Recommended Specs:

  • Capacity: 32GB is the sweet spot for modern gaming and creative work.
  • Speed: DDR5 6000MHz or higher is recommended for the latest platforms.
  • Tip: Always use two sticks (e.g., 2x16GB) to enable Dual-Channel mode.

4. Storage - NVMe SSD

Determines how fast your games load and how quickly your system boots up.

Recommended Storage:

  • Type: NVMe M.2 SSD (Gen 4 or Gen 5).
  • Capacity: 2TB is ideal as modern games can take up over 100GB each.
  • Top Pick: Samsung 990 Pro or WD Black SN850X.

5. Motherboard - The Backbone

Connects all your high-end parts together and provides power delivery.

Buying Tips:

  • Chipset: Z790 (Intel) or X670/B650 (AMD) for the best features.
  • Connectivity: Look for Wi-Fi 6E/7 and at least 2-3 M.2 slots for storage.
  • Important: Ensure the socket matches your CPU (e.g., AM5 for Ryzen 7000).
